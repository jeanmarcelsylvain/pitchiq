# User Pain Points — PitchIQ

Research method: 12 user interviews with competitive youth players ages 15–21, supplemented by subreddit analysis (r/bootroom, r/youngsoccer) and review mining of existing apps (Hudl Focus, StatSports, Strava).

---

## Pain Point 1: No Personal Stats, Only Team Stats

**Severity:** Critical  
**Frequency:** 11/12 players interviewed

Youth clubs and high schools that do track stats focus on team outcomes, not individual metrics. Players have no objective record of their own performance — goals, pass accuracy, distance, or sprint speed — across a season.

**User quote:** *"My coach tracks team possession but I have no idea what my own pass accuracy was. I'm guessing."*

**Opportunity:** Be the first product to make individual, longitudinal performance tracking dead simple for youth players.

---

## Pain Point 2: Self-Improvement is Invisible

**Severity:** High  
**Frequency:** 10/12 players interviewed

Players feel they're improving but have no way to measure it. Without a baseline, every season resets. Players can't tell whether a skill like sprint speed or pass accuracy has actually changed.

**User quote:** *"I've been training for three years but I can't tell you if I'm actually faster than I was last year."*

**Opportunity:** Show players a visible, data-backed improvement arc over time. Make progress feel real.

---

## Pain Point 3: No Context for Self-Assessment

**Severity:** High  
**Frequency:** 9/12 players interviewed

After a match, players rely on memory and coach feedback to understand how they played. Memory is unreliable and biased. Coach feedback is often aggregate ("good press today") rather than individual.

**User quote:** *"I gave myself a 7 after the game but I honestly don't know if that's right. I can't remember exactly how many chances I created."*

**Opportunity:** Make match logging the habit right after the final whistle, while the game is fresh. Structured data entry forces players to evaluate themselves accurately.

---

## Pain Point 4: Goal-Setting Without Accountability

**Severity:** Medium  
**Frequency:** 8/12 players interviewed

Players set verbal or mental goals at the start of a season ("I want to score 10 goals") but have no system to track progress. Goals get forgotten. Progress is never measured.

**User quote:** *"I told myself I'd have 15 assists this season. We're in March and I genuinely don't know if I'm on track."*

**Opportunity:** A goals tracker with progress bars and milestone notifications turns vague ambition into a structured, accountable system.

---

## Pain Point 5: Recruiting is a Black Box

**Severity:** High (among ECNL/higher-level players)  
**Frequency:** 7/12 players interviewed

College coaches ask recruits for performance data, but players have nothing to share beyond a highlight reel and a hand-typed one-pager. Players who can't quantify their game lose ground to players who can.

**User quote:** *"A coach asked me what my average pass completion was. I had to say I didn't know. That was embarrassing."*

**Opportunity:** PitchIQ's player profile becomes a data-backed recruiting asset. Future feature: one-click shareable profile link.

---

## Pain Point 6: Fragmented Tracking Across Tools

**Severity:** Medium  
**Frequency:** 6/12 players interviewed

The minority of players who do track anything use a patchwork: Notes app for match summaries, Google Sheets for stats, Strava for run fitness, Instagram DMs for coach feedback. Nothing talks to anything else.

**User quote:** *"I have a Google Sheet, a Notes doc, and a voice memo habit. It's a mess and I never review any of it."*

**Opportunity:** PitchIQ becomes the single home for all soccer performance data — the system of record for a player's development.

---

## Pain Point 7: No Insight Layer

**Severity:** Medium  
**Frequency:** 5/12 players interviewed

Even players who do track stats manually end up with raw numbers they don't know how to interpret. Data without context is noise.

**User quote:** *"I know my pass accuracy was 78% last game and 82% the game before. But I don't know if that's good, what it means, or what to do about it."*

**Opportunity:** The Insights Engine is PitchIQ's highest-differentiation feature. Automatic, plain-English interpretations of performance data turn stats into coaching.
