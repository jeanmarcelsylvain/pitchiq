# User Journey Map — PitchIQ

**Persona:** Maya Chen (ECNL Competitor, age 17)  
**Scenario:** First week using PitchIQ through 3-month habit formation

---

## Stage 1: Awareness & Sign-Up

**Trigger:** Maya's teammate mentions PitchIQ on the bus ride home after a tough loss. She Googles it, lands on pitchiq.dev.

| Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Reads headline, scans features | Curious, skeptical | Social proof: "Used by ECNL players" badge |
| Demo dashboard | Clicks "Try Demo" | Excited — sees her position's metrics | No friction — demo before sign-up |
| Sign-up flow | Google OAuth, 1 click | Relieved it's fast | Confirm email instantly |
| Onboarding | Sets up player profile | Engaged, personalizing | Ask 5 questions max |

**Pain point here:** Long sign-up flows kill conversion. PitchIQ resolves this with Google OAuth and a 5-field profile setup.

---

## Stage 2: Onboarding (Day 1–2)

**Trigger:** She just got home from her first match after signing up.

| Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard (empty state) | Sees prompt: "Log your first match" | Slightly unsure | Clear empty state CTA with example data |
| Match log form | Enters stats from tonight's game | Focused, recalling details | Auto-save as she types |
| First insight | Sees "You played 90 mins at CM — log 3 matches to unlock your trend insights." | Intrigued | Progress unlocking creates motivation |
| First goal set | Creates "Score 10 goals" goal | Excited | Pre-fill suggested goals based on position |

**Aha Moment:** When she sees her first match card with a rating badge and stats laid out cleanly — it feels real, not like a spreadsheet.

---

## Stage 3: First 30 Days — Habit Formation

**Trigger:** Plays 4 matches over the month. Logs each one within 24 hours.

| Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Analytics page | Clicks "Pass Accuracy" trend | Surprised to see it's inconsistent | "Your lowest accuracy games were away matches — is there a pattern?" |
| Insights | Reads "Sprint Speed Up 4%" | Proud, validated | Shareable insight card for Instagram stories |
| Goals tracker | Sees she's at 40% on "10 goals" | Motivated | Milestone notification: "Halfway there!" email |
| Weekly email | Receives "Your Week in Review" digest | Feels cared for | Automated, personalized with real stats |

**Critical action:** Opening the app unprompted after 2 weeks (non-prompted return visit) is the key retention signal.

---

## Stage 4: Deep Engagement (Month 2–3)

**Trigger:** Receives interest from a college coach. Wants to send something beyond a highlight reel.

| Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile page | Reviews season stats | Proud, wants to share | One-click shareable profile (v1.1 feature) |
| Analytics | Shows coach on phone | Confident | PDF export of season summary |
| Insights | Shows "CAM vs CM performance gap" insight | Informed, strategic | Discussion with coach about position |
| Goals | Updates current values manually | Committed | Auto-sync goals from match logs (v2 feature) |

---

## Stage 5: Advocacy

**Trigger:** Her pass accuracy trend clearly shows a 9-point improvement over 10 weeks.

| Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Achievement notification | Receives "85% pass accuracy milestone" alert | Ecstatic | Share to Instagram → organic growth |
| Team chat | Screenshots her stats summary and shares it | Proud | Referral prompt: "Invite a teammate, both get early access to Recruiting Mode" |
| Review prompt | Asked to rate PitchIQ | Positive | In-app NPS + App Store review request |

---

## Moments That Matter

1. **First match logged** — The moment a player sees their stats rendered visually for the first time
2. **First insight generated** — When the app tells them something they didn't know about themselves
3. **First goal milestone hit** — Progress becomes tangible and gamified
4. **First time they share a stat** — PitchIQ becomes a social identity object, not just a private tool

---

## Journey Map Gaps (Future Work)

- **Coach loop:** No mechanism for a coach to view or annotate a player's data
- **Calendar integration:** No visibility into match schedule to prompt logging
- **Training-to-match correlation:** We log training but don't yet show whether training sessions improve match outcomes
